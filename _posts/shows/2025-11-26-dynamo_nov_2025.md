Dark streets. Dangerous secrets. And… spontaneous singing? 🎶<!--more-->
Step into a smoky world of mystery, where every song, every crime, and every heartbreak is made up on the spot.

The Basic Pitches bring you PITCH BLACK — a completely improvised film noir musical where the only script is your imagination. Expect shady detectives, glamorous suspects, and a few musical twists that no one saw coming.

Join us for a night of shadows and spontaneous stories.
